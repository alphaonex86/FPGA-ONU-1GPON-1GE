## Before use

- DE25-Standard jumpers: HSMC 12V header on pins 2-3 (the SFP cages' buck), JP3 at 3.3 V, HSMC JTAG on bypass (the
  card does not loop TDI to TDO).
- The card hangs east of the host on the HSMC connector alone: M3 standoffs from H1/H2 down to the bench, or SFP
  insertion (up to 40 N) levers the SMT connector.
- BOSA (U4): lead order and spacing estimated from a photo, not measured (ordered as is, operator 2026-11-05); check
  them against an X100DG before soldering it.
