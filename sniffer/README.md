# GPON sniffer

A passive tap on one ONU's drop fibre: both directions of that ONU's GPON traffic,
filtered to what concerns it, dumped with the direction of every record. Simple and
cheap: one small card on the DE25-Nano 40-pin GPIO. Origin: `../common/sniffer/sniffer-GPON.jpg`.

Versus the FPGA ONU (operator, 2026-11-02): the ONU + OLT proxy of this repo sits in the path
and can intervene in the packets; the sniffer cannot touch anything, it only listens, but it
dumps without complications (no activation, no cloning, the stock ONU keeps its link).

## Decisions (operator, 2026-11-02)

- Customer side, not provider: the OLT is the ISP's, remote and out of our hands. The tap goes
  between the wall fibre outlet and our own ONU; the sniffer must stay invisible to the ISP
  (passive, never emits; our ONU receives about 4 dB less, see Coupler).
- The link: about 5 km of fibre from the ISP; our ONU reports
  -17 to -27 dBm received. -27 dBm is already at a class B+ ONU's sensitivity (-27/-28 dBm),
  so any optical tap that takes light from the downstream can drop our ONU off the PON.
  Range across installations: the sniffer is for homes at -23 dBm or better (Coupler).
- Tap: two 1x2 couplers back to back in our ONU's drop (Coupler below), so the two
  directions arrive on two fibres (no WDM) and the upstream holds our ONU's bursts only,
  at one constant power.
- Optics: two BOSAs, receive only. Downstream: an ONU BOSA RX (X100DG, 1490 nm, APD-TIA).
  Upstream: the OLT BOSA XL-BCB2111-LS-02 RX (1310 nm, 1.25 Gb/s APD-TIA, -32 dBm). Each
  into a SY88349NDL limiting amp. No laser driver is fitted: the sniffer cannot emit into
  the PON, by construction. SFP modules were dropped: no raw GPON ONU module without a MAC
  on AliExpress (only "sticks" with a MAC); raw ones exist at Optcore/FiberMall/PLANET.
- Platform: an ECP5 SerDes card on the DE25-Nano GPIO (the `../de25-nano/pcb` bridge
  plan; the operator owns a DE25-Nano). The ECP5 deserialises; the Agilex runs the existing GTC deframer, FEC, AES
  and the filter; the HPS writes the dump.

## Coupler (operator, 2026-11-02)

Two 1x2 back to back: wall - [common 1x2 #1 50 %] - [90 % 1x2 #2 common] - ONU. The free leg of #1
is the downstream copy, the 10 % leg of #2 the upstream copy (one 1x2 alone taps one direction:
the light entering a leg leaves by the common port). #1 is a 50:50, #2 a 90:10: the
operator has several of each (unbalanced 1x2 for building GPON networks).
- ONU: about 4 dB less (3.5 + 0.5). The -17 to -27 dBm range is across installations, so the
  sniffer fits a home whose ONU reports -23 dBm or better without the tap (it then stays at
  -27 dBm or better, a class B+ ONU's sensitivity).
- Downstream: the sniffer gets the ONU's tapped level + 0.5 dB, at least -26.5 dBm there, above
  the APD's -28 dBm.
- Upstream: the ONU emits +0.5 to +5 dBm, the sniffer sees about -10 to -5.5 dBm, near an APD's
  overload (-8 dBm typical for an OLT receiver): a 5 dB SC attenuator on the upstream tap fibre.

## DE25-Nano: verified (2026-11-02)

- GPIO headers are 3.3 V HVIO: 100 MHz SDR or 200 Mb/s DDR per pin (Altera Agilex 5 GPIO
  architecture brief); the HVIO clock input reaches the I/O PLL up to 156.25 MHz (data sheet).
- So the card sends DDR: DS 2.48832 Gb/s on 16 pins + US 1.24416 Gb/s on 8 pins, 155.52 Mb/s
  per pin (78 % of the limit) on a 77.76 MHz forwarded clock. With CCLK/MOSI/MISO/SN/PROGRAMN
  (slave-SPI configuration from the Agilex) and I2C: 36 of the 40 pins of GPIO_0.
- The Agilex A5EB013 is the DE25-Standard's density (138K LE), where the GTC/FEC/AES already
  meet timing; the HPS has the GbE port and the microSD for the dump.
- The two ECP5 bridge boards in `../de25-nano/pcb` send 155.52 MHz SDR: above the 100 MHz HVIO limit.

## Card (`pcb/sniffer`, generated by `../common/pcb/gen`)

Routed 2026-11-02: KRT, then close_opens on the last 5 nets; KiCad: 0 errors, 0 unconnected.

ECP5 LFE5UM-25F (as the bridge boards), SerDes RX only: CH0 downstream (CDR), CH1 upstream
(lock-to-reference on the 155.52 MHz VCXO, which the ECP5 locks to the downstream like the
ONU board, so each burst needs only a phase pick). Each BOSA: receive can only, into a
SY88349NDL burst-mode limiting amp (SD mode, auto reset, squelch), its own TPS61391 APD bias
(DAC trim from an AD5593R, which also reads both APD currents = optical power at the tap).
Upstream coupling 220 pF (tau 11 ns), downstream 100 nF.

## Filter (downstream; the upstream is already this ONU only)

The ONU is named by its serial number. From the PLOAMs: Assign_ONU-ID (serial ->
ONU-ID), Assign_Alloc-ID, Configure_Port-ID (OMCI); from OMCI: the GEM Port CTPs
(data Port-IDs). Kept: PLOAMs to that ONU-ID or broadcast, the BWmap entries of its
Alloc-IDs, the GEM frames of its Port-IDs.

## RTL (`../common/hw/spinal/pon/sniffer`, test `pon.sniffer.SnifferTest`)

Vendor-neutral SpinalHDL, one line byte per clock per direction; no AES in hardware.
- `SnifferDs` (311.04 MHz): the GTC deframer (FEC included), then the filter: PLOAMs to
  `onuId` or broadcast (all with `allPloams`), BWmap entries and GEM fragments by two
  4096-bit maps (Alloc-ID, Port-ID) the HPS writes. GEM stays ciphered; its record carries
  the crypto-counter, the software decrypts.
- `SnifferUs` (155.52 MHz): delimiter hunt on any bit alignment, descramble from the BIP,
  one record per burst until 8 squelched (zero) line bytes.
- `RecordWriter`: the record stream; a record is kept or dropped whole, a full data FIFO
  cuts its tail (flag bit 7); drop and truncation counters.

Record: `0xA0|kind`, flags, payload length (16 LE), time (64 LE), payload.

| kind | payload | flags |
|------|---------|-------|
| 0 PLOAM | 12 bytes | 0: bad CRC |
| 1 allocation | 7 bytes as on the line | 0: bad CRC |
| 2 GEM fragment | Port-ID 16, PLI 16, counter 48, data | 2..0: PTI, 3: cut |
| 3 upstream burst | BIP, ONU-ID, Ind, allocations | 0: maxBytes reached |

ECP5 (`ecp5/`, `sh sniffer/ecp5/build.sh` -> `out/ecp5/sniffer.bit`, yosys + nextpnr + trellis):
`sniffer_ecp5.v` (DCU, resets, PLL, DDR pins) around `SnifferBridge` (test `pon.sniffer.SnifferBridgeTest`);
`gen.py` writes the DCU parameters and the LPF from the routed board. All in the VCXO's domain
(124.416 MHz, CH0's TX clock): CH1 is read there; the downstream crosses a 16-word FIFO whose
fill is the phase detector of the VCXO loop (PI every 132 us, sigma-delta on VCXO_PWM;
`ecp5/vcxo_loop_model.py`: locked to 0.5 word in 14 s from 10 ppm). Upstream: one sample per
pair, the centre of isolated 1- or 3-sample runs (an edge on a sample reads either bit).
Timing (speed 6): VCXO domain 135.6 MHz for 124.4, out 125 for 77.8, DS 255 for 124.4.

Still to write: the Agilex wrapper (DDR capture, x4 / x2 serialisers, time base, both
streams to the HPS).

## Dump

pcapng, one interface per direction (0 = downstream, 1 = upstream), hardware
timestamps; one packet per record.

## Open

- SY88349NDL stock (LCSC not reachable from here) and its SD level resistor (5.1k start).
- Lead order of the X100DG BOSA's RX can (to measure); XL-BCB2111 order from its drawing.
- XL-BCB2111 connector: SC/UPC in the title, SC/APC on the drawing.
- CH1 between bursts: with the line squelched the DCU sees LOS and its phase detector stops
  (`PDEN_SEL`); the 2x pick needs the DCO to stay on the reference frequency there, to measure.
- DCU bit order: the first received bit taken in D0 (as LiteICLink's 8b10b path); a reversed
  lane would show as a deframer that never syncs.
- VCXO: its pull range must exceed its initial error plus ageing (SiT3821: pick >= +-100 ppm).
- ECP5 in the BOM is the C grade (LFE5UM-25F-6BG381C): the I grade is the industrial one.
- 5 dB attenuator part number; connectors (drop SC/APC, BOSA pigtails per part).
